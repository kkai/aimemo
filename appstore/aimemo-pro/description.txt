ai-Memo Pro turns speech into text on your iPhone or iPad. Tap record, talk, and the transcript appears while you speak. Every transcript is saved to a history you can search.

TWO ENGINES
You choose the engine in Settings and can change it at any time.

- Whisper: OpenAI's Whisper model, running on your device through whisper.cpp. It detects the language by itself and transcribes about 99 of them. Good for interviews, meetings and technical discussions.
- Apple Speech: Apple's built-in speech recognition. It starts quickly and suits short notes.

FOUR WHISPER MODELS
All four are multilingual and ship inside the app, so there is nothing to download later.
- Tiny (31 MB): fastest
- Base (57 MB): the default, a good balance
- Small (181 MB): more accurate
- Medium (514 MB): most accurate, slowest

RECORDING HISTORY
Transcripts are saved automatically. Search them, open them again, and swipe or long-press to share or copy one.

LANGUAGES
- Automatic detection, or pin a language when you record short clips
- Translate to English: speak any supported language and get English text, still offline
- Custom vocabulary for names, jargon and acronyms

PRIVACY
Whisper runs entirely on your device and needs no internet connection, so your audio and text stay on your iPhone or iPad. Apple Speech also runs on the device where iOS supports your language. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics.

ALSO INCLUDED
- Live waveform and recording timer, with the detected language next to it
- Copy with one tap, or share from the recording screen
- Landscape on iPhone and iPad
- Dark interface
- Metal GPU acceleration
- One-time purchase, with no ads and no subscription
