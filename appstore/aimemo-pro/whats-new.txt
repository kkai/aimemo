ai-Memo Pro 2.6 adds file transcription and handles interruptions during a recording.

• Transcribe a file: pick an audio or video file in the app, or share one to ai-Memo Pro from another app. The transcript is saved to your history under the file's name.
• Pause and resume while you record.
• A phone call, Siri or an alarm now pauses the recording instead of quietly ending capture. It resumes when iOS allows it, and otherwise the app offers Resume.
• Switching headphones no longer stops a recording.
• Leaving the app pauses the recording and tells you why. Keep the app open while you record.
• Apple Speech used to stop by itself after about a minute. It now carries on.
• The screen stays awake while you record, and the app tells you when no sound reaches the microphone.
• Share as Markdown with timestamps, or as SRT and WebVTT subtitles. This works from the recording screen and from any saved recording.
• Summaries: on a device with Apple Intelligence, you can summarize a finished transcript on the device. The summary is saved with the recording.
• Siri and Shortcuts: Start a Memo, Stop the Memo and Transcribe a File. Shortcuts can put them on the Action button.
• VoiceOver labels, Dynamic Type and larger touch targets.
• Fixed: the transcript shrank to two lines after a recording.
