ai-Memo Pro 2.5 gives you control over the language.

• Translate to English: speak any of the roughly 99 supported languages and get English text back. It still runs on your device.
• Language picker: let Whisper detect the language, or pin one when you record short clips.
• The detected language now shows next to the recording timer.
• Custom vocabulary: add the names, jargon and acronyms it used to guess at.
• Share from your history: swipe or long-press any recording to share or copy it.
• You can share straight from the recording screen, and the app works in landscape on iPhone and iPad.
• Long recordings: transcription now runs in short windows, so memory use stays flat and the live text keeps up on meeting-length recordings.
