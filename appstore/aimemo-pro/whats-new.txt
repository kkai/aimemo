ai-Memo Pro 2.5 puts you in control of the language:

• Translate to English — speak any of the ~99 supported languages and get English text back, still entirely on your device.
• Language picker — let Whisper detect the language, or pin one when you are recording short clips.
• See what was detected — the language now appears next to the recording timer.
• Custom vocabulary — teach it the names, jargon and acronyms it used to guess at.
• Share from your history — swipe or long-press any recording to share or copy it.
• Share straight from the recording screen, and landscape support on iPhone and iPad.
• Long recordings stay live — transcription now runs in short windows, so memory stays flat and the live text no longer falls behind on meeting-length recordings.
