ai-Memo turns speech into text on your iPhone or iPad. Tap record, talk, and the transcript appears while you speak.

TWO ENGINES
You choose the engine in Settings and can change it at any time.

- Whisper: OpenAI's Whisper model, running on your device through whisper.cpp. It detects the language by itself and transcribes about 99 of them. Good for interviews, meetings and technical discussions.
- Apple Speech: Apple's built-in speech recognition. It starts quickly and suits short notes.

TRANSCRIBE A FILE
Pick an audio or video file, or share one to ai-Memo from another app. Whisper transcribes it on the device and shows its progress. You can cancel at any time.

PAUSE AND INTERRUPTIONS
- Pause and resume while you record
- A call, Siri or an alarm pauses the recording. It resumes when iOS allows it, and otherwise the app offers Resume
- The screen stays awake while you record, and the app tells you when no sound reaches the microphone
- Keep the app open while you record: leaving it pauses the recording

LANGUAGES
- Automatic detection, or pin a language when you record short clips
- Translate to English: speak any supported language and get English text, still offline
- Custom vocabulary for names, jargon and acronyms

PRIVACY
Whisper runs entirely on your device and needs no internet connection, so your audio and text stay on your iPhone or iPad. Apple Speech runs on the device where iOS supports your language; for other languages iOS sends the audio to Apple. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics.

ALSO INCLUDED
- Live waveform and recording timer, with the detected language next to it
- Copy with one tap, or share as text, Markdown with timestamps, SRT or WebVTT
- On-device summaries on devices with Apple Intelligence
- Siri and Shortcuts actions: start a memo, stop it, transcribe a file
- VoiceOver and Dynamic Type
- Landscape on iPhone and iPad
- Dark interface
- Metal GPU acceleration

ai-Memo Pro adds a recording history and a choice of four Whisper models.
