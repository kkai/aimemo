ai-Memo transforms your voice into accurate text using advanced on-device AI. Choose between two powerful transcription engines to match your needs.

DUAL ENGINE TRANSCRIPTION
Switch between Whisper AI and Apple Speech with a single tap:

- Whisper AI: Industry-leading accuracy powered by OpenAI's whisper.cpp, running entirely on your device. Now multilingual — automatically detects and transcribes around 99 languages. Ideal for interviews, meetings, and technical discussions.
- Apple Speech: Fast recognition with multi-language support. Perfect for quick notes and everyday use.

PRIVACY FIRST
All transcription happens on your device. Your voice data never leaves your iPhone or iPad. No internet connection required for Whisper models.
