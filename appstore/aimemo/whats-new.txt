ai-Memo 2.6 is about trust: the recording keeps going, and you keep what it heard.

• Transcribe a file: pick an audio or video file in the app, or share one to ai-Memo from Files, Voice Memos or Mail.
• Pause and resume while you record.
• A phone call, Siri or an alarm pauses the recording instead of ending it. Switching headphones no longer stops it either.
• Apple Speech used to stop by itself after about a minute. It now carries on.
• The screen stays awake while you record, and the app tells you when no sound reaches the microphone.
• Share as Markdown with timestamps, or as SRT and WebVTT subtitles for video editing.
• Summary: on an iPhone or iPad with Apple Intelligence, a summary card appears under the finished transcript.
• Siri and Shortcuts: "Start a memo", "Stop the memo" and "Transcribe a file". Put one on the Action button or in Control Center.
• VoiceOver, Dynamic Type and bigger touch targets throughout.
• Fixed: the transcript shrank to two lines after a take.
